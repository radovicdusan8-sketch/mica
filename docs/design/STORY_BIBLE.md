# STORY BIBLE — ***Severed Blade*** *(alt / subtitle: The Fivefold Blade)*

> **Companion docs (2026-07-31 world-build pass):** [The Opening — cinematic → Act 0 → The Fall](OPENING.md) · [World of Amagiri — realm & clans](WORLD_AMAGIRI.md) · [Magic & the Demon](MAGIC_AND_DEMON.md) · [The 13 Stops, deepened](CITIES.md)

> A cinematic, stylized samurai action game about a dismissed son who must reforge his blade, master the four elements, and find his exiled brother — to reclaim the clan that betrayed their father and to avenge the mother whose death tore the family apart.

**Status:** Second draft — story spine locked with the user. Names and beats below are settled unless marked _[PROPOSED]_ (still open to change).

---

## 1. The One-Liner

A lone swordsman, denied his birthright and robbed of his father, walks a road of trials to awaken the four elemental powers sleeping in his one-of-a-kind katana — and to find the brother the world branded a mother-killer — so the two can return, expose the truth, and take back the clan from the elder who stole everything.

---

## 2. Tone & Identity

The four pillars, and what they mean in practice:

- **Mythic / supernatural** — spirits, oni, cursed steel, and forbidden "demon" power. The world is haunted, not historical.
- **Stylized, not photoreal** — bold colors, strong silhouettes, painterly skies. Closer to *Sekiro/Nioh* than to realism.
  - **Refinement (locked via concept art, 2026-07-31):** *environments and cities* lean **stylized & painterly** — atmospheric, overkill-detailed, ominous ("something's coming"). *Characters* are rendered **grounded and realistic** — AAA-style, à la Nioh / Ghost of Tsushima. **Grounded heroes in a painted mythic world.**
- **Invented land, not real Japan** — our own clans, map, and myths. No Mongols, no history to answer to. _(Keeps us out of Ghost of Tsushima's lane entirely.)_
- **Operatic, flashy combat** — named techniques, dramatic finishers, larger-than-life duels. Zoro energy.

**Emotional target ("aura drama"):** every major beat ends on a hook. Grief sits under every victory — the journey was meant for *two*, and he walks it alone.

---

## 3. The World

- **The land:** *Amagiri* — "Heaven's Mist" _[name PROPOSED]_ — a fractured realm of mountain valleys, lantern-lit cities, misted forests, and old battlefields where spirits linger. Once unified, now a patchwork of clans holding their own domains.
- **The clans & the elements:** Long ago, warriors learned to bind an element into their steel. Most bloodlines can channel **one**; the truly gifted, **two**. To hold all four is the stuff of legend — said to belong only to the one who would unite Amagiri again. This is why Ryuma's blade matters, and why the powerful fear it.
- **The Four Elements:** **Wind · Water · Fire · Lightning.**
- **The Fifth, forbidden power:** **Demon.** Spoken of only in myth — a power that devours as it grants. The world believes it a story to frighten children. It is not. *(See §5 — the twist.)*

---

## 4. The Sword — Magic System = Progression System

- **Ryuma's katana is one-of-one:** the only blade capable of holding **all four** elements. He begins with **none** awakened.
- He **learns each element on the road**, from a teacher / trial in a different region. **4 elements = 4 chapters.**
- **Element order:** **Wind → Water → Fire → Lightning.** Meaningful: he ends by mastering the two elements his *father* wielded (Fire & Lightning), symbolically stepping into and then beyond his father — right before the demon truth lands.
- His **father's blade** channeled **two** — Fire and Lightning — marking him a master, and quietly showing how rare his son is.
- **The second part of the blade** — a legendary fragment fused into Ryuma's own katana by the **master swordsmith** — unlocks the **Demon** power, the mythic fifth.
- **The demon's cost (real tradeoff):** it is immense power on a knife's edge. Overuse risks the bound spirit slipping away forever — or overtaking Ryuma. Mechanically: a high-risk resource with dramatic consequences if leaned on too hard.

**How a power is gained & grown:**
1. **Learn the base skill** — each teacher grants the *core* ability of their element (e.g., Wind's *Severing Gale*). This is the one-time unlock that opens the power.
2. **Open the skill tree** — with the base skill comes a **branching tree** of abilities and upgrades for that element.
3. **You choose** — the player spends **skill points** (earned from bosses, trials, and exploration) to unlock the branches *they* want. Every playthrough builds a different Ryuma.

**5 elements = 5 skill trees**, awakened at Old Tomoe's forge and shaped by the player. The interlude cities are where you farm points and *master* a tree before the next element arrives. Story and mechanics are one machine.

## 5. The Cast

- **Protagonist — *Ryuma*** — the younger son. Quiet, disciplined, deadly; carries a grief he refuses to show. Zoro-like: stoic strength over swagger. Dismissed as heir by a clan that saw only his youth. Bears the one blade that can hold all four elements — and, in time, the fifth.
- **The Father — *Master Hidetora*** _[name PROPOSED]_ — aging leader of the clan and Ryuma's only teacher. Wielded Fire and Lightning. Betrayed and mortally wounded; his last act is to send Ryuma after the brother.
- **The Mother — *Lady Kaede*** — murdered years before the story begins. She uncovered the truth no one else could see: that the trusted council-man **Sadao was a planted agent of House Kiri**, the rival clan. Before she could warn Hidetora, Sadao had her killed to silence her — then framed her death on her own son. Her fate is the family's original wound. **Her spirit is the "demon" in the second blade fragment** — and she carries both truths she died holding. *(The twist — see §6.)*
- **The Brother — *Kaito*** — the elder son. Once the clan's brightest blade and rightful heir — until he was **framed for their mother's death** and exiled in disgrace. Believes his family let him fall. Now a rogue swordsman with a fearsome, bitter reputation. The reunion is the heart of the game.
- **The Betrayer — *Sadao*** — **the family's oldest and dearest friend.** Not a scheming councilman the player is primed to distrust, but Hidetora's confidant of decades, an uncle to the boys, the man Kaede trusted — warm, kind, always the first to help and the last to leave. That love is the disguise, and it is total: the player is meant to *like* him and never suspect him, so the reveal lands like a knife. The truth beneath it: **he is a deep-cover agent — likely the heir — of House Kiri**, the rival clan that has been Amagiri's weakest for its entire history and has plotted for generations to take the realm from the shadows. He gave his whole life to the mask.
  - **Two murders, two methods.** He **killed Lady Kaede with his own hands** — intimate, clean, perfect — when she discovered his allegiance. But **Hidetora he had killed by hired mercenaries**, because a two-element master is death to approach *and* because Sadao needed to be seen elsewhere, innocent. The hired blades botched it — which is the one crack that lets Hidetora live long enough to pass his dying words to Ryuma.
  - **The alibi.** Moments after the killing, Sadao **arrives first at the burning house**, grieving harder than anyone, pulling Ryuma from the flames and comforting the orphaned boy — the single act that erases him from suspicion for the clan *and the player.* He then steps into the clan's leadership not as a usurper but as the heartbroken friend holding a wounded house together.
  - Cold, patient, politically brilliant. To the end he wears the mask — *"I am the strong hand this realm needed"* — and will say so to Ryuma's face. Endgame antagonist; the reveal that the beloved friend is the author of everything — *and* that he is House Kiri — is the game's most unexpected twist, saved for the reunion and detonated fully at the finale. *(House Kiri: see [World of Amagiri §4–5](WORLD_AMAGIRI.md).)*
- **The Swordsmith — *Old Tomoe*** _[name PROPOSED]_ — the father's oldest friend; the one hand alive who can awaken the blade's powers. He was there the night Kaede died, and helped seal her spirit into the fragment. Ryuma's guide and the game's "hub" character; keeper of the game's deepest secret.
- **The Four Teachers** — each a living piece of Master Hidetora's past, and each agrees to help Ryuma **because of the father, not the son.** Full bonds & backstories in §5b.

## 5b. The Four Teachers — Father-Bonds & Backstories

**Unifying rule:** every teacher knew Master Hidetora, and each helps Ryuma *because of that bond* — so the journey is Ryuma walking through his father's whole life. Their four relationships span it: youth-rival → war-comrade → sworn-brother → old master.

- **🌪️ Hazuki — Wind (Takanoha).** Trained beside Hidetora in their youth, blades matched as equals — until a duel took her eyes. Hidetora carried her from the field and swore she'd never want for anything; she withdrew to keep the bells of Takanoha, learning to "see" by the wind. **Helps because she owes his father her life** — and Ryuma's grip is Hidetora's ghost. She once met Lady Kaede, and drops the first hint that her death was no accident.
- **🌊 Isen — Water (Ukishima).** Bled beside Hidetora in the border wars. When Isen's blade failed and men died, Hidetora shouldered the blame *with* him and brought him home. Isen laid down his sword to ferry souls across the black water in penance. **Helps because that debt is never paid.** Warned Hidetora the council had rotted; was not heeded.
- **🔥 Genta — Fire (Homura).** Hidetora's **sworn brother** by blood-oath. When Sadao's coup came, Genta — cornered, threatened, afraid — fled instead of standing with his brother, and has drowned in shame and drink at the forge since. **Helps to atone**, handing his fire to the son he abandoned.
- **⚡ The Hermit — Lightning (Narukami).** Before the clan, he was **Hidetora's own master** — the first to put lightning in his hands — later exiled for forbidden lore: the binding of souls into steel. **Helps because Ryuma is his student's son**, and he alone remembers what the "demon" truly is. His knowledge is the reason Kaede's soul could be bound at all — a guilt he carries up the storm-peak.

## 6. The Story — Act Structure

### THE OPENING — Cold Open → Act 0 → The Fall
*Full beat sheet in [OPENING.md](OPENING.md). Summary:*

**Cold Open (cinematic) — "The Night of Falling Leaves."** *Years before the game.* The player sees House Kurogane whole, once: Hidetora, Kaede, the golden heir Kaito, and a young Ryuma. **Lady Kaede** discovers a truth she was never meant to — proof of a betrayer at the heart of the home — but before she can warn Hidetora she is **silenced by an unseen hand** and her murder staged to frame **Kaito**, the strong heir, who is cast out in disgrace. *We never see the face behind it.* In secret, **Old Tomoe** and the grieving father seal what remains of Kaede — her spirit — into a **blade fragment**, a taboo the frightened call "demon-work." The one who holds the shattered house together is **Sadao** — the family's dearest friend, an uncle to the boys — the villain *introduced as a beloved ally, never as a suspect.* The more the player loves him, the harder the truth will cut. **Title card: *SEVERED BLADE.*** *(Withheld from the player until the reunion/finale: that Sadao did it, that the "demon" is Kaede, and that Sadao serves House Kiri.)*

**Act 0 (playable) — "Before the Fall."** *Present day; Ryuma a young man, blade un-awakened.* Three missions bond the player to **Hidetora** before he dies — and teach the world's power so the player knows what they'll pursue: *(1)* **"The Lesson"** — training with father (combat tutorial); he ignites **Fire** then **Lightning** and names the ladder — most hold one element, masters two, and one legendary blade once held *all four* and united the realm. *(2)* **"The Lord's Road"** — ride out and see why the realm loves Hidetora; meet Tomoe, Ayame, and the hawk Kaze in the good times. *(3)* **"The Council's Doubt"** — the aging Hidetora is doubted and **Ryuma rejected as heir** while Sadao patiently poisons the room. *(Maps to build Milestones 0–3 — Ryuma with zero magic, his true starting state.)*

**The Fall (hook into the main game) — a murder dressed as a fire.** Across Act 0, **Hidetora has been quietly doubting Kaito's guilt** and hunting the truth alone. Returning from the last mission, **a note** hands him the final proof — Kaito is innocent, Kaede was murdered to bury it, the hand is still inside the clan. He knows what his wife knew the night she died. Resolving to arm himself and set it right — and to tell Ryuma once it's safe — he goes for his gear, and in that instant **Sadao's hired mercenaries cut him down** and **set the house alight** to sell it as a tragic accident. *(Sadao killed Kaede himself, years ago, cleanly; Hidetora — a two-element master — he has killed by hired blades, so he can be seen elsewhere and innocent.)* The mercenaries botch it — the one crack in the plan — leaving Hidetora bleeding but alive just long enough. **Ryuma is away** on an errand to a nearby village; he returns to screaming and smoke, sprints into the burning house, and finds his father bleeding out. He grips his son's hand — *everything stops for a breath* — and gives his last words: **"Find your brother… Ryuma,"** then, unable to finish, **"He knows the tr—"** — and dies, the truth half-spoken. Moments later **Sadao arrives first at the fire**, grieving hardest, pulling Ryuma from the flames — the perfect alibi that erases him from suspicion for the clan *and the player.* **Ryuma never sees a killer, only a friend who came to help.** He carries two broken fragments into exile with his un-awakened blade. *The mystery is the engine: the whole game is Ryuma finishing his father's last sentence — and the parent-parallel (both die the instant they act on the truth) makes two tragedies one designed wound. The beloved-friend villain is withheld until the reunion. (Full sequence in [OPENING.md](OPENING.md).)*

### ACT I — The Road (Wind & Water)
Ryuma travels out from clan lands into Amagiri's cities and wilds. He finds **Old Tomoe**, learns what his blade truly is, and begins the pilgrimage of the elements — earning **Wind**, then **Water**, each from a teacher, each a self-contained dramatic chapter with a trial or boss, each dropping a breadcrumb about his brother and hints that his family's tragedies were no accidents.

### ACT II — The Truth in the Steel (Fire, Lightning & the fragment)
Ryuma earns **Fire** and **Lightning** — his father's two elements, now surpassed. A **mysterious figure** delivers the **second part of the blade**; Tomoe, cornered by Ryuma's questions, begins to reveal what it holds and the cost it carries. The conspiracy sharpens: the same hand that framed Kaito for their mother's death also killed their father.
*Hook: to awaken the fifth power, Ryuma must accept a truth that frightens even Tomoe.*

### ACT III — The Reunion & the Revelation (Demon)
Ryuma finds **Kaito** — hardened, bitter, certain his own blood abandoned him (their first meeting may be a duel, or a fight side-by-side against a common threat). The turn comes when Ryuma awakens the fragment and the **demon truth lands: it is their murdered mother**, her spirit bound in the steel — and through her, the full truth pours out. **Kaito was framed. Sadao killed her. Sadao holds the clan.** Exile becomes purpose. Two brothers, torn apart *on purpose*, choose to walk home together — their mother's blade between them.

### ACT IV — The Return (twist → triumph)
The brothers march on the clan. The elders who followed Sadao are not all villains — many believed the lie. The finale confronts **Sadao**, who argues to the last that he *saved* them.

**The twist that turns triumphant:** at the height of the confrontation — with Ryuma tempted to spend the demon power recklessly and lose his mother forever — Kaede's bound spirit instead does what she died trying to do: **she reveals the truth to the whole council.** And she carries *two* bombshells, the ones she died holding: first, that **Sadao murdered her and framed Kaito** — and second, the deeper one, that **Sadao is no Kurogane at all, but a planted agent of House Kiri**, the scorned rival clan that engineered the whole fall from the shadows. The council does not merely learn it followed a usurper — it learns it was **conquered from outside and never knew it.** The very "demon" the clan feared becomes the witness that clears Kaito and damns Sadao. The family's greatest wound becomes the weapon that wins. Shame turns to fury; the deceived elders turn on the outsider who played them. Sadao falls; the clan is reclaimed; **Kaito is vindicated at last.** In the quiet after, Kaede's spirit — her purpose finally served — passes on in peace, and the brothers stand together in the seat their father meant for them both.

**Earned grief note:** triumphant, but not clean. The father is still gone; the mother, having spoken her truth, is finally at rest and lost a second time. The clan is restored — and forever changed.

## 9. The Road of Amagiri — Full Itinerary (13 stops)

The journey alternates **element cities** (learn a power) with **interlude cities** (master the power you have, earn resources, advance the story). Powers are deliberately spaced out so each is mastered before the next arrives; the Demon comes only after two full arcs beyond Lightning. _(See the journey map rendered in chat.)_

## 10. Recurring Characters (connective tissue for the interludes)

- **Sōjirō Amano — "the Laughing Blade"** — a wandering swordsman who duels Ryuma repeatedly across the journey (first at Sakaimichi; decisive duel at Shirasagi). A grinning, brilliant duelist who fights for joy; a measure of Ryuma's growth, and likely an ally by the end. Very Zoro.
- **Kuroba — "the Ironhound"** — Sadao's relentless hunter on Ryuma's trail (introduced at Kareno; last stand at Sekimon). Cold and tireless, he never stops tracking. Recurring mini-antagonist before the finale.
- **The Mysterious Figure** — glimpsed across cities; finally delivers the second fragment at Narukami.
- **Ayame — the Spy** — Lady Kaede's former handmaid *and* Ryuma's childhood friend, who stayed inside Kurogane-jō after the fall, still loyal to the family and never believing Kaito's guilt. From within Sadao's own household she feeds Ryuma the truth — troop movements, plans, whispers of the mother's death. First contact at Higan. Her closeness to both the mother and Ryuma makes her intel — and her danger — personal.
- **Kaze — the Loyal Bird** — Ryuma's messenger hawk (name means "Wind"), **a gift from his father** in boyhood, carrying letters between Ryuma and Ayame. In-game: delivers news that opens new objectives and lets the world (and home) react to Ryuma's growing legend. Ties to the hawks of Takanoha.

## 8. What This Buys Us for Building

This bible maps *directly* onto the milestone ladder from our plan:
- **Milestones 0–3** (movement, combat, one enemy, health) build **Ryuma** and his blade with **zero magic** — exactly his starting state in the Prologue.
- **Milestone 4** ("The Legend") becomes the **first elemental teacher / trial (Wind)** — our first cinematic boss encounter, drawn straight from Act I.
- **Milestone 5** ("Growth") becomes the **first element awakened** in the swordsmith's ability tree.

The story doesn't sit in a drawer — it *is* the build order.
