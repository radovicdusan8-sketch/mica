# THE OPENING — Cold-Open Cinematic → Act 0 → The Fall

> Companion to the [Story Bible](STORY_BIBLE.md) §6 and [World of Amagiri](WORLD_AMAGIRI.md). This doc details the game's **first hour**: the framing cinematic, the playable missions that make you love Ryuma's father before you lose him, and the world-power exposition woven through — all restructured per the design goal of *earning* Hidetora's death.

**Status:** First pass (2026-07-31). Story-locked at beat level; staging open.

**Design goal (user's words, paraphrased):** open on a cinematic that frames the brother and introduces the villain and the drama; then give the player real *missions with the father* so they get attached before he dies — so the death lands hard; and along the way, teach the world's power system so a new player knows what they're chasing.

---

## The Three Jobs of the Opening

1. **Make you love Hidetora** — so his death is a wound, not a plot beat. Achieved with playable father-son missions, not a cutscene.
2. **Teach the world's power** — the elements, the ladder (one / two / four), and the Fivefold legend — so the player knows the *pursuit* ahead.
3. **Plant the villain and the wound** — the cold open shows Kaito framed and introduces Sadao; the truth of *who* and *why* stays buried for the player to uncover.

---

## PART 1 — COLD OPEN (cinematic) · "The Night of Falling Leaves"

*Fully cinematic, or lightly interactive (walk-and-watch). Set **years before** the main game.*

- **Establish the family whole, once.** A single warm image of House Kurogane intact: Master **Hidetora**, Lady **Kaede**, the golden elder son **Kaito**, and a young **Ryuma** in his brother's shadow. This is the only time the player sees the family unbroken — it must ache in hindsight.
- **Kaede's discovery.** Kaede has found something she was never meant to — proof of a betrayer at the very heart of the home. *We are not shown what, or who.* She moves to warn Hidetora, urgent and afraid.
- **The murder & the frame.** Before she can, she is silenced: killed by an **unseen hand** and staged so the blade points at **Kaito**, the strong heir. We see the deed done in shadow — never the face behind it.
- **The condemnation.** Kaito — the clan's brightest blade — is branded his mother's murderer and cast out in disgrace. His last look is at a family that did not fight for him. Young Ryuma watches his brother swallowed by the mist, his father broken, his mother gone in a night.
- **Introduce Sadao — but do not accuse him.** Among those who steady the grieving house is the trusted council-man **Sadao**: calm, kind, always the reasonable voice. *He does not gloat — he mourns with them.* The player meets the villain without knowing he is the villain. The whole game is spent deciding whether to trust that grief.

**What the cold open plants:** the central wound (Kaede dead, Kaito framed by an unseen hand), a *face to suspect* (Sadao) without confirmation, and the emotional debt the finale will repay. **Withheld from the player until later:** that Sadao did it, that he serves House Kiri, and that the "demon" to come is Kaede herself.

> **Title card after the cold open:** ***SEVERED BLADE.*** Then: *"Years later…"*

## PART 2 — ACT 0 · "Before the Fall" (playable — bond with the father)

*Present day. Ryuma is a young man. **No element awakened** — his blade is bare steel. These missions are the combat tutorials (build Milestones 0–3) **and** the emotional groundwork.*

> **The running thread — Hidetora's doubt.** Beneath every Act 0 mission, a quiet second story: **Hidetora has never fully believed Kaito killed his mother.** Each mission tightens that doubt by a notch — a look, a discrepancy, a witness — so the player feels the father inching toward the very truth that got his wife killed. He keeps it close, guarding Ryuma from it, chasing it alone. This is the fuse. It burns down to **the note** (below) — and the moment he is *certain*, he dies for it, exactly as Kaede did. *The player should only realize in hindsight that they watched a man walk into the same trap that took his wife.*

### Mission 1 — "The Lesson" · training with Hidetora
- **Play:** movement, camera, light/heavy attacks, dodge, parry — taught *diegetically* as Hidetora spars with his son in the Iron Castle's training yard.
- **Bond:** Hidetora is hard but warm; he believes in Ryuma where the council sees only his youth. A line that will echo at his death.
- **Doubt (notch 1 — the wound that won't close):** in a quiet moment after the spar, Hidetora speaks of Kaito with a grief that isn't a murderer's father's grief — he has kept something of his exiled son's, and a half-said line reveals he has never truly believed the verdict. The seed.
- **World-power exposition (the key beat):** Hidetora ends the lesson by showing what Ryuma cannot yet do — he ignites his blade with **Fire**, then **Lightning**, the *two* elements that mark him a master. Ryuma (and the player) sees elemental power for the first time.
  - *Hidetora, teaching:* most warriors who carry the gift hold **one** element; a rare few, **two**. And once, long ago, a single blade held **all four** — and united the realm. "No one has since. They say the world is not ready for another." *(The pursuit is now named: the player knows the ladder and the legend they will climb.)*

### Mission 2 — "The Lord's Road" · ride out with your father
- **Play:** traversal + first real combat against human threats (bandits, or a wilds spirit menacing a village on Kurogane land). Escort/defend structure.
- **Bond:** the player sees *why the realm loves Hidetora* — he is a just, beloved lord who protects common folk himself. Ryuma fights at his side and earns a rare word of pride.
- **Cast seeding:** meet **Old Tomoe** the smith (who tends Hidetora's blade and eyes Ryuma's ordinary katana strangely — a seed), childhood friend **Ayame**, and the messenger hawk **Kaze**, Hidetora's boyhood gift to Ryuma. All the people who will carry the later game are planted here, in the good times.
- **Doubt (notch 2 — the story doesn't add up):** out in the domain, Hidetora crosses a loose thread from that night — an old retainer, a witness, a detail of the murder that never fit the official account. The player sees him go quiet, then quietly start *pulling*. He shares none of it with Ryuma.
- **World texture:** villagers speak of the clans and the mist and the dead — the realm's haunted nature established through play, not lore-dumps.

### Mission 3 — "The Council's Doubt" · the shadow moves
- **Play:** a slower, tension mission inside Kurogane-jō — a formal council, a quieter kind of danger.
- **Drama:** the aging Hidetora is doubted; the council rejects **Ryuma as heir**. **Sadao** works the room — reasonable, patient, sympathetic — planting the case that the old lord is failing and the clan needs a steadier hand. Ryuma feels something *wrong* in Sadao he cannot name (the player, remembering the cold open, can).
- **Grief note:** Kaede's absence sits over the hall; Kaito's name is a wound no one will speak. The family's fracture is present tense.
- **Doubt (notch 3 — almost certain):** Hidetora's private hunt reaches the edge of proof. He now suspects not just that Kaito was innocent, but that the truth points *inside these very walls* — and the player, remembering the cold open, may feel where his eyes keep landing. He is one piece of evidence away. He tells Ryuma nothing, to keep him safe.
- **World-power callback:** the debate itself turns on power — a clan with no strong heir is prey in Amagiri. The stakes of the elemental world are made concrete: weakness here is death.

> **Pacing:** three missions is the target — enough to *earn* the father, not so many the player waits too long for the game to turn. Optional: a short fourth beat (a private father-son moment the night before the Fall) to twist the knife.

## PART 3 — THE FALL *(the hook into the main game)*

*No public coup, no battle in the great hall. A quiet murder dressed as an accident — devastating precisely because the player has lived three missions beside this man, and because it echoes, beat for beat, the death of the mother in the cold open.*

### The note — Hidetora becomes certain
- Returning home from the last mission, Hidetora receives **a note** — the final piece his private hunt was missing. It confirms everything: **Kaito never killed his mother. Kaede was murdered to bury the truth. And the hand behind it is still inside the clan.** He now knows exactly what his wife knew the night she died. *(Source deliberately open — see threads — a strong option: the same **Mysterious Figure** who will later deliver the fragment has been trying to reach the family for years.)*
- **The fatal instinct — to protect, then to share.** He will not put this on Ryuma cold. He resolves to arm himself, ride out, and set it right — to reach Kaito, or to confront the truth — and to tell his son everything *once he is sure it is safe.* He goes to **grab his gear.** That instinct to act is what gets him killed — the same instinct that killed Kaede. The moment either parent *moved* on the truth, the hand struck.

### The murder — cold blood, staged as fire
- In his own home, reaching for his blade, **Hidetora is cut down in cold blood** by an assassin who was always close enough. To bury it, the killers **set the house alight** — the death will be told as a tragic accident, an old lord lost to a fire. Clean. Deniable. No coup, no accusation, nothing for the realm to rally against. *(This is the shadow-agent's signature — exactly how a patient spy removes a lord: not with an army, but with an "accident.")*

### Ryuma arrives too late
- **Ryuma is not there.** He had gone to a **nearby village for goods** — an ordinary errand, the last ordinary thing he will ever do. On the road back he **hears screaming** and sees smoke, then fire, over the rooftops — *his home.* He runs.
- He **sprints into the burning house** and finds his father on the floor, bleeding out amid the flames. Ryuma is screaming — begging him to hold on, to *survive* — hauling at him, useless with panic and grief.
- **The hand.** With the last of his strength Hidetora finds his son's hand and grips it. **Everything stops for a breath** — the fire, the noise, all of it falls away to the two of them.
- **The last words:** *"Find your brother… Ryuma."* A shudder. He tries to give the rest — *"He knows the tr—"* — and cannot finish it. His hand goes slack. **He is gone**, the truth dying half-spoken in his mouth.

### What Ryuma is left with
- **The villain is not revealed.** Ryuma never sees the hand that did this. He knows only what his own eyes tell him — *his father was bleeding; this was no accident* — and two unfinished fragments: **find your brother**, and **he knows the tr—.** The world will call it a fire. Ryuma knows it was murder. He does not know by whom.
- **Exile.** With his home ash, his father dead, and the clan closing around a "steadying hand" he has no power to challenge, Ryuma leaves Kurogane-jō — carrying his father's last words and his own **un-awakened** blade. Alone, on a road built from a half-sentence.
- **The mystery is the engine.** Everything after this is Ryuma chasing the end of that broken word. *"He knows the truth"* points to **Kaito** — and it is only when Ryuma finally reaches his brother that the real truth lands, for Ryuma **and for the player at the same moment:** Kaito was framed, their mother was murdered, and the calm council-man who mourned with them did all of it. *(The deepest layer — that he is no Kurogane at all, but House Kiri — is held back further still, for the finale.)*

> **Why this is stronger than a public betrayal:** the mystery gives the player a *question* to carry, not just a grief; the disguised murder keeps the villain hidden so the reunion reveal can detonate; and the parent-parallel (both die the instant they act on the truth) turns two separate tragedies into one designed wound. The father's love — *protect the boy, tell him only when it's safe* — is the very thing that robs Ryuma of the answer, so the whole game becomes Ryuma finishing his father's last sentence.

## How the Opening Serves the Whole

- **Emotional engine:** every later father-bond beat (the four teachers all help *because of Hidetora*) now lands harder, because the player *knew* him.
- **The pursuit is legible:** by the end of Act 0 the player understands elements, the one/two/four ladder, and the Fivefold legend — so the whole road (Wind → Water → Fire → Lightning → the forbidden fifth) reads as a clear climb toward the legend Hidetora named.
- **The villain is a slow-release:** the cold open plants a *face to suspect* (Sadao) but never confirms him; the father's death deepens the mystery instead of naming a culprit; the player assembles the truth across the road, it lands at the reunion, and the deepest layer (Kiri) detonates at the finale.
- **The father's last sentence is the spine:** *"He knows the tr—"* is the question the whole game answers. Every breadcrumb is Ryuma finishing his father's broken word.
- **Maps to the build ladder:** Act 0's three missions are exactly Milestones 0–3 (movement, combat, one enemy, health) — Ryuma with **zero magic**, which *is* his starting state. The story and the build order remain one machine.

## Open Threads (opening-level)
- **Who sends the note** that makes Hidetora certain — anonymous informant, a surfacing piece of Kaede's hidden evidence, Ayame, or (recommended) the **Mysterious Figure** who later delivers the fragment, tying the enigma together from the first hour.
- **Does Ryuma fight beside his father anywhere in Act 0**, or is the burning-house arrival the first time steel really matters to him personally?
- **The staging of "everything stops"** at the death — literal slow-motion / desaturation / silence, vs. a subtler hold. A signature moment worth prototyping.
- **Cold-open interactivity:** fully cinematic vs. walk-and-watch playable — a staging choice.
- **A fourth Act-0 beat** (the quiet night before) — include or cut for pace.
- **Does young Ryuma play in the cold open**, or is it purely observed? (Affects tutorial placement.)
