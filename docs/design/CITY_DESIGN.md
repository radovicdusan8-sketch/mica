# CITY DESIGN — Severed Blade

> The **design layer** on top of [CITIES.md](CITIES.md) (which holds each stop's story role). This doc defines *how each place looks, feels, and plays* — silhouette, architecture, palette, light, layout/flow, soundscape, setpieces, and gameplay function — enough to brief environment art and blockout. Built on the locked itinerary.

**Status:** First design pass (2026-07-31). Shared language + the first two locations (Kurogane-jō, Takanoha) in full; template below for the rest. Iterating in road order.

**Art direction (locked):** environments are **stylized & painterly** — atmospheric, *overkill*-detailed, ominous ("something's coming"). Bold silhouettes, painted skies, strong mood over realism. *Grounded heroes in a painted mythic world.* The world should feel a half-step haunted everywhere the mist gathers.

---

## 0. Shared Environment Language

- **Mist as the world's signature.** Amagiri ("Heaven's Mist") reads through fog, god-rays, and depth-haze in every scene — thin in safe places, thick and close where the veil (and the dead) press in. Mist is the mood dial *and* a Kiri visual pun.
- **Silhouette-first.** Every location earns one **iconic landmark silhouette** readable from far off (the Cliff of Bells, the Iron Castle, the Storm Shrine). Players should navigate by landmark.
- **Element = palette + weather.** Each element region carries its color and sky (see [Character Design §0](CHARACTER_DESIGN.md)): Wind = pale/white/skyblue and moving air; Water = blue/teal and fog/rain; Fire = crimson/ember and heat-haze; Lightning = violet/storm; Kurogane = charcoal/iron; Kiri-touched places = drained, grey, *too quiet.*
- **Two-track cities.** **Element cities** = trial/boss architecture (a shrine, a peak, a haunted harbor) built around a setpiece arena. **Interlude cities** = lived-in social hubs (markets, festivals, castles) built around vendors, NPCs, and farming skill points.
- **Sadao's shadow is visible.** As the road goes on, occupied cities show it environmentally: garrison banners over clan ones, curfew-empty streets, boarded shrines, the drained "Kiri quiet." The world visibly falls behind you.
- **Painted set-dressing density.** Lean into *overkill* detail — lanterns, prayer-cloth, weathering, layered god-rays — so each frame reads like concept art. Optimize with painterly distance-cards, not by simplifying the hero framing.

**Structure (locked 2026-07-31):**
- **Open regions, not linear stages.** Each stop is a **crafted city/setpiece embedded in an explorable open region** (Elden-Ring-ish) — the wilds *between* cities are real, roamable space for exploration, optional combat, and farming skill points. So every location below now carries a **Region** (the open area around it) alongside the city core. The city is the dense heart; the region is the breathing room.
- **Elements are combat-first — no traversal-gating.** Movement stays consistent everywhere; the world is **not** a Metroidvania locked behind Wind-glides or Water-swims. Instead, the **skill trees unlock a few long-range / ranged-cast abilities** (e.g. a wind blade thrown at distance, a lightning bolt-dash) — that's a core thing skill points buy. Design arenas and regions for a swordsman who gains *some* ranged options, not for power-gated traversal.

**Per-location design template** *(used below and for every future city):*
> **Pillar** (one feeling) · **Region** (open area around it) · **Landmark silhouette** · **Architecture & materials** · **Palette & light** · **Layout & player flow** · **Soundscape** · **Setpiece / boss arena** · **NPCs & vendors** · **Gameplay function** · **Art/blockout notes**

## P — KUROGANE-JŌ · The Iron Castle *(Prologue home)*

- **Pillar:** *warmth about to be taken.* The one place that must feel like **home** before it burns — so the fire guts the player, not just Ryuma.
- **Region — the Kurogane Vale:** the central crossroads lowland the whole realm's roads run through; farms, the nearby village of Ryuma's errand, forge-hamlets. Gentle, ordinary, *safe* — the only region that ever feels like peace. (In the finale it returns occupied and grim.)
- **Landmark silhouette:** a black-iron keep on a crossroads rise, forge-smoke always trailing from its stacks — the realm's dark, steady heart.
- **Architecture & materials:** blackened iron and dark timber; the great **ancestral forge** at its core (glowing, sacred); training yards, a lord's hall, family quarters. Lived-in, not a fortress-maze — corridors the player will *remember* when they're on fire.
- **Palette & light:** charcoal and iron warmed by **forge-orange and hearth-gold** — a deliberately cozy, protected glow in Act 0. For The Fall: the same palette inverted — hearth-gold becomes **arson-orange**, warm shadows become smoke.
- **Layout & player flow:** compact and legible — this is the tutorial home. The training yard (Mission 1), the hall (Mission 3 council), the family rooms, and the road out to the nearby village (Ryuma's errand). Small enough to know by heart; that familiarity is the weapon in The Fall.
- **Soundscape:** ring of the forge, wind in the banners, family voices — a home. In The Fall: fire-roar, collapsing timber, and then the terrible quiet of the hand-grip.
- **Setpiece:** **The Fall** — the burning house, the dying-father hand scene, Sadao's arrival. Fire as a scripted, cinematic hazard-space, not a combat arena.
- **NPCs & vendors:** Hidetora, Tomoe, Ayame, Kaze, Sadao, the council — the whole "good days" cast, seeded here so their later fates land.
- **Gameplay function:** onboarding — movement, combat, camera (build Milestones 0–3), all **zero-magic.** Emotional groundwork. Returns as the transformed **finale siege** (hollowed, occupied — see [HOME_FRONT.md](HOME_FRONT.md)).
- **Art/blockout notes:** build the Act 0 "warm" dressing and the Fall/finale "violated" dressing as **two lighting/prop passes on one layout** — the recognition (same rooms, ruined) is the point. Prototype the fire hazard-space early; it's a signature scene.

## 1 — TAKANOHA · The Cliff of Bells *(Element — Wind · Milestone 4 target)*

- **Pillar:** *held breath, high and holy.* Beauty on the edge of a killing drop — awe and vertigo.
- **Region — the Takanoha Highlands:** wind-scoured cliff-country above the clouds — ridge trails, hawk-roosts, ruined wind-shrines, rope-bridge chasms. Open but *vertiginous*; exploration rewards nerve. Sōra hermits and yōkai of the high air.
- **Landmark silhouette:** a sheer cliff-shrine strung with **thousands of prayer-bells and hawk-lines**, pale against a vast painted sky; wind-torn banners; a tengu-shrine crowning the peak.
- **Architecture & materials:** weather-bleached wood and rope, Sōra bell-towers and roost-platforms, swaying bridges over cloud. Everything **light, layered, and moving** — a place built for wind, not war.
- **Palette & light:** whites, pale grey, sky-blue; high-altitude clarity shot with drifting cloud and god-rays. The realm's brightest, cleanest look — a deliberate contrast to the charcoal home just lost.
- **Layout & player flow:** **vertical.** Ascend the cliff via bell-platforms and rope-bridges, wind gusts as environmental push/hazard (teaching air *before* granting it). The climb *is* the level; the tengu shrine at the summit is the goal. Verticality showcases the coming Wind moveset.
- **Soundscape:** the whole place is instrument — ten thousand bells, hawk-cries, wind that rises to a roar near the summit. Bells go silent right before the boss (dread beat).
- **Setpiece / boss arena:** the **Tengu** on a wind-scoured shrine platform, gusts sweeping the arena — the game's **first cinematic boss**, the trial that awakens **Wind (Severing Gale).** Arena uses wind push/pull as a mechanic.
- **NPCs & vendors:** **Hazuki**, blind bell-keeper (Wind teacher), tends the shrine; a few reclusive Sōra bell-tenders; hawk-keepers who tie to **Kaze.**
- **Gameplay function:** first **element awakened** + first boss (build Milestones 4–5); opens the **Wind skill tree**; drops the **first crack in the lie** about Kaede via Hazuki.
- **Art/blockout notes:** sell the height — deep painterly cloud-parallax below every platform. Wind must be *visible* (streaming cloth, bells, hawk-feathers, grass) so the invisible force reads. This is the "wow, look at this world" beat right after the grief of The Fall — pour the painterly detail in.

## 2 — SAKAIMICHI · The Crossroads Market *(Interlude hub — Master Wind)*

- **Pillar:** *everything for sale, danger under the color.* The loud, teeming place where the whole realm rubs shoulders — and where a fugitive can vanish or be sold out.
- **Region — the Crossroads Plains:** the open trade-heartland where every road branches — waystations, roadside shrines, bandit camps, merchant caravans. The map's connective hub-region and a prime early spot to roam and farm skill points.
- **Landmark silhouette:** a great tiered crossroads gate where the roads meet, and the ever-smoking chimney of **Tomoe's forge.**
- **Architecture & materials:** sprawling wooden market town — stacked stalls, awnings, banners, tangled alleys — with the solid, warm forge as its anchor building.
- **Palette & light:** warm earth, lantern-gold, road-dust; a riot of banner-color against a grey working sky. Busier and dirtier than Takanoha — the ordinary world.
- **Layout & player flow:** a horizontal hub-maze — central market plaza (vendors), **Tomoe's forge** (upgrade & awaken hub), a duel-ring, and back-alleys for stealth and bounties. The player learns the interlude loop here.
- **Soundscape:** crowd-roar, haggling, street music, hammer-ring — and a sudden hush when bounty-hunters clock Ryuma's face.
- **Setpiece:** no boss — the **first Rival duel** with **Sōjirō** in the market ring, and the beat where **Sadao brands Ryuma a fugitive** and the bounty board lights up.
- **NPCs & vendors:** **Old Tomoe** (the hub — gear, awaken the Wind tree, lore), merchants, info-brokers, **Sōjirō's** grinning debut, bounty-posters. A natural spot for the **first letter from Sadao** to arrive.
- **Gameplay function:** the **economy / gear / mastery hub** — farm skill points, master the Wind tree, learn the vendor-upgrade loop; the bounty on Ryuma begins (the world starts reacting to his legend).
- **Art/blockout notes:** overkill market density; make the forge a warm sanctuary island in the churn. This is the player's first "town" — it must feel alive.

## 3 — UKISHIMA · The Floating Harbor *(Element — Water)*

- **Pillar:** *beautiful and mournful.* A city on black water where the living ferry the dead.
- **Region — the Fogbound Coast:** reed-marsh, tidal flats, drowned villages and boat-lanes lost in fog — explorable by skiff, haunted by drowned things. The veil feels thin out here.
- **Landmark silhouette:** a vast tiered harbor of boardwalks and moored boats over black water, lantern-lit and half-swallowed by fog; the soul-ferry piers.
- **Architecture & materials:** Nagi stilt-houses and boardwalks, net-draped hulls, lacquer and rope; everything doubled in the still black water's reflection.
- **Palette & light:** deep blue, teal, black water, fog-grey, lantern-gold reflections, drifting rain — mournful and gorgeous.
- **Layout & player flow:** semi-horizontal over water — a boardwalk maze, boat-hops, tidal zones that shift; the boss lurks out in the ferry-lanes.
- **Soundscape:** lapping water, fog-bells, distant funeral chants, creaking boats — and the rising wail of the drowned.
- **Setpiece / boss:** the **Funayūrei** swarming the fog-lanes — the trial that awakens **Water (Returning Tide);** tide and water as arena hazards.
- **NPCs & vendors:** **Isen** (Water teacher, the penitent soul-ferryman), wavering Nagi harbor-lords already bending to Sadao, boat-vendors. Isen reveals the father **suspected betrayal.**
- **Gameplay function:** second element + Water skill tree; deepens the conspiracy from "accident" to "betrayal"; the soul-ferry quietly seeds the demon lore to come.
- **Art/blockout notes:** reflections and fog *are* the mood — nail the black-water mirror. Foreshadow Yomotsu's thin veil here.

## 4 — HIGAN · The Lantern City *(Interlude — Master Water · occupied)*

- **Pillar:** *gorgeous danger in a mask.* A festival of the dead that is also enemy ground.
- **Region — the Lantern Reaches:** a river-plain of drifting lantern-rivers, grave-fields, and festival hamlets — beautiful, and threaded with Sadao's checkpoints and patrols.
- **Landmark silhouette:** a whole city drowned in floating lanterns, a great river of light — with garrison banners hung over the festival.
- **Architecture & materials:** dense festival city — paper lanterns, shrine-avenues, stages, masked crowds — with Sadao's soldiers and checkpoints woven cold through the warmth.
- **Palette & light:** night; rivers of warm lantern-gold on black water; painted festival color — draining to the **Kiri "too-quiet" grey** at the garrison's edges.
- **Layout & player flow:** a crowd-dense **incognito hub** — blend into masked crowds, dodge patrols, take rooftop routes; social infiltration over open combat.
- **Soundscape:** festival music and bells riding over an undertow of marching boots — the dead honored above, the living watched below.
- **Setpiece:** no boss — an **incognito infiltration** of the enemy's city, **first contact with Ayame**, and the **second Rival bout** with Sōjirō.
- **NPCs & vendors:** **Ayame** (spy, first contact — begins feeding truth via **Kaze**), an **ally** met in the crowd, black-market vendors, garrison patrols.
- **Gameplay function:** master the Water tree; turns the plot toward active conspiracy; opens the **letters/objective system** (Ayame's truth vs. Sadao's comforting letters — see [HOME_FRONT.md](HOME_FRONT.md)).
- **Art/blockout notes:** the lantern-rivers are the signature image; stage festival-warmth and garrison-cold in the *same frame* to make the occupation felt.

## 5 — HOMURA · The Ember Forge *(Element — Fire)*

- **Pillar:** *ominous grandeur — heat, pride, and shame.* A city built into living fire.
- **Region — the Cinder Reach:** volcanic foothills of lava-fields, ash-forests, mining camps and ruined furnaces — heat hazards and fire-yōkai across Kaen territory.
- **Landmark silhouette:** a city terraced into active smelters and lava-lit foundries; chimney-stacks and firelight against a black-and-orange sky; the great Ember Forge at its heart.
- **Architecture & materials:** Kaen furnace-city — iron, stone, scorched timber; foundry halls, smelter-terraces, channels of molten metal. Heavy, industrial, martial.
- **Palette & light:** crimson, ember-orange, black iron, heat-haze — the hottest, heaviest look in the game.
- **Layout & player flow:** a vertical descent through the foundry toward the deep smelters where the boss waits; lava and heat as environmental hazards; hammering industry all around.
- **Soundscape:** a thousand hammers, roaring furnaces, hissing steam — building to the boss's furnace-roar.
- **Setpiece / boss:** the **Fire Oni** loosed in the deep smelters — the trial that awakens **Fire (Ember Fang)**, the first of the father's two elements; a lava-arena.
- **NPCs & vendors:** **Genta** (Fire teacher — the sworn brother who *ran*, drowning his shame at the forge), proud Kaen furnace-lords who resent Sadao but haven't moved, weapon-vendors.
- **Gameplay function:** third element + Fire skill tree; the road's rawest father-bond confession; establishes Fire as the **family's shared flame** (Hidetora carried it, Genta teaches it, Kaito wields it).
- **Art/blockout notes:** heat-haze and firelight everywhere; make Genta's corner of the forge a pocket of drunken shame amid the industry's roaring pride.
