# OPENING CINEMATIC — Shot-by-Shot Storyboard / Animatic

> A directable shot list for the opening cinematic — the cold open "The Night of Falling Leaves," the title drop, and the smash into game-feel. Turns [OPENING.md](OPENING.md)'s beats into camera, action, timing, and audio, with a **ready-to-run AI video-gen prompt per shot** so it can be built into an animatic. Consistent with the locked realistic art direction.

**Status:** First pass (2026-07-31).

**How to use this:** generate each shot as a 5–8s clip (Higgs/Flux `generate_video`, or hand to an animator), then cut them in sequence — that's your opening animatic. Keep character/wardrobe consistent by reusing your approved **Ryuma** and city concepts as reference images where the tool allows.

**Runtime target:** ~2:00. **Look (locked):** photoreal, high-fidelity, AAA (Ghost of Tsushima / Nioh / Elden Ring; UE5). **Mood:** warm memory curdling into a single cold night. **Palette motif:** autumn maple-red (Kaede = "maple") + hearth-gold, draining to smoke and mist.
**Audio spine:** a single lonely instrument (shakuhachi flute / biwa) over silence; one low sustained dread-note enters at the murder and never fully leaves; hard silence around the title.

## ACT: THE NIGHT OF FALLING LEAVES *(memory — warm, then cold)*

### Shot 1 — The World *(0:00–0:10)*
- **Camera:** slow aerial push over misted mountain valleys toward **Kurogane-jō**, forge-smoke trailing, at amber dusk.
- **Purpose:** establish Amagiri — the mythic, haunted scale of the world.
- **Audio:** distant temple bell; lone flute.
- *Gen-prompt:* "Cinematic slow aerial drone push over vast misty mountain valleys at amber dusk toward a colossal black-iron feudal-fantasy castle-city trailing forge-smoke, volumetric fog and god-rays, photorealistic Unreal Engine 5, Ghost of Tsushima look, hyper-detailed, epic scale, NOT anime, NOT cartoon."

### Shot 2 — The Family, Whole *(0:10–0:22)*
- **Camera:** slow push-in through a warm lantern-lit hall to a family at a low table — **Hidetora, Kaede,** young **Kaito** (teen), younger **Ryuma** (child).
- **Purpose:** the only time we ever see them unbroken. Make it ache.
- **Audio:** muffled warm laughter, fire crackle.
- *Gen-prompt:* "Cinematic slow push-in on a warm lantern-lit traditional feudal-Japanese-fantasy hall interior at night, a noble family of four sharing a quiet warm meal by firelight, soft golden light, realistic faces and fabric, photorealistic Unreal Engine 5 render, shallow depth of field, tender intimate mood, NOT anime, NOT cartoon."

### Shot 3 — She Notices *(0:22–0:32)*
- **Camera:** on **Kaede** — her smile fades as her eyes catch something off-frame (a document, an overheard word). Maple leaves drift past a lattice window.
- **Purpose:** the discovery. Dread enters the warmth.
- **Audio:** laughter drops out; the dread-note is born, faint.
- *Gen-prompt:* "Cinematic close-up of a noble woman by candlelight, her warm smile slowly fading to alarm as she notices something off-screen, red maple leaves drifting past a wooden lattice window behind her, realistic skin and eyes, moody low-key lighting, photorealistic film still, NOT anime, NOT cartoon."

### Shot 4 — The Warning *(0:32–0:42)*
- **Camera:** handheld follow — Kaede moving fast and afraid through lantern-lit corridors, sleeve and leaves trailing.
- **Purpose:** she races to warn Hidetora. Rising panic.
- **Audio:** urgent footsteps, breath, the dread-note swelling.
- *Gen-prompt:* "Cinematic handheld tracking shot following a frightened noblewoman hurrying through dim lantern-lit wooden castle corridors at night, kimono and red maple leaves trailing, flickering shadows, urgent tense mood, photorealistic Unreal Engine 5, motion blur, NOT anime, NOT cartoon."

### Shot 5 — The Unseen Hand *(0:42–0:50)* — *do NOT show the killer's face*
- **Camera:** she stops short; a shadow falls across her; cut to a low angle of a dropped lantern rolling, a spray of red maple leaves settling on the floor, a shadow-hand withdrawing. Smash to black on impact.
- **Purpose:** the murder — implied, faceless. Preserve the mystery.
- **Audio:** a single sharp sound, then total silence.
- *Gen-prompt:* "Cinematic ominous low-angle shot in a dark castle corridor: a lantern falling and rolling on wooden floor scattering red maple leaves, a menacing shadow of an unseen figure cast on the wall withdrawing, no visible face, dread and violence implied not shown, photorealistic, high contrast chiaroscuro, NOT anime, NOT cartoon."

### Shot 6 — The Frame *(0:50–1:02)*
- **Camera:** cold morning. **Kaito** (teen) held before the assembled clan, a bloodied blade forced into evidence against him, faces turning away. Push in on his uncomprehending face.
- **Purpose:** the golden heir branded his mother's killer.
- **Audio:** low murmur of a condemning crowd; the flute returns, mournful.
- *Gen-prompt:* "Cinematic wide-then-push-in shot, cold grey morning in a feudal-fantasy castle courtyard, a shocked young man accused before a grim assembled clan, guards restraining him, faces turning away in judgment, realistic, somber desaturated palette, photorealistic Unreal Engine 5, NOT anime, NOT cartoon."

### Shot 7 — The Exile *(1:02–1:14)*
- **Camera:** wide — **Kaito** walking away alone into thick mist through the castle gate; he tears the clan crest from his shoulder and lets it fall. In the foreground, child **Ryuma** reaches after him, held back by a hand.
- **Purpose:** the brothers severed. The wound.
- **Audio:** wind, a single bell toll.
- *Gen-prompt:* "Cinematic wide shot, a lone exiled young warrior walking away into thick grey mist through a massive castle gate, dropping a torn clan emblem on the ground behind him, a small child reaching after him in the foreground held back, melancholy, photorealistic Unreal Engine 5, volumetric fog, epic sorrowful mood, NOT anime, NOT cartoon."

## SMASH TO GAME-FEEL *("Years later…" — world look + how it plays)*

### Shot 11 — The World, Now *(1:44–1:54)*
- **Camera:** grand reveal — grown **Ryuma** small on a misty mountain road, **Kaze** the hawk wheeling overhead, a vast Amagiri vista and a distant city landmark beyond.
- **Purpose:** show the scale and beauty of the world the player will roam (open regions).
- **Audio:** the lone flute, now with a hopeful/steely undertone.
- *Gen-prompt:* "Cinematic sweeping wide shot, a lone weathered samurai in a dark indigo kimono and charcoal haori walking a misty mountain road, a hawk wheeling overhead, a vast breathtaking fantasy valley with a distant castle-city on the horizon, photorealistic Unreal Engine 5, Ghost of Tsushima look, volumetric fog, epic scale, NOT anime, NOT cartoon."

### Shot 12 — How It Feels to Fight *(1:54–2:04)*
- **Camera:** dynamic — Ryuma draws the bare katana and delivers one grounded, weighty, decisive strike against a foe (no magic — his Prologue state); quick cuts convey the combat's heft and precision.
- **Purpose:** sell the *game feel* — grounded, deliberate, Nioh/Sekiro weight.
- **Audio:** the clean, brutal sound of one drawn cut; music surges.
- *Gen-prompt:* "Cinematic dynamic action shot, a lone samurai drawing a katana and delivering one powerful precise decisive sword strike against an enemy on a misty road, grounded weighty realistic motion, dust and cloth movement, photorealistic Unreal Engine 5, Ghost of Tsushima combat feel, motion blur, NOT anime, NOT cartoon."

### Shot 13 — The Road Ahead *(2:04–2:12)*
- **Camera:** Ryuma sheathes, breath fogging, and looks to the distant city; hold, then hard cut to black / first gameplay.
- **Purpose:** hand the player the goal and the reins.
- **Audio:** wind; a faint echo of Hidetora's whisper — *"Find your brother…"*
- *Gen-prompt:* "Cinematic shot of a weathered samurai sheathing his katana and looking toward a distant fantasy city on a misty horizon, breath fogging in cold air, resolute, photorealistic Unreal Engine 5, golden-hour rim light, epic hopeful-somber mood, NOT anime, NOT cartoon."

## Editing & Production Notes
- **Consistency:** feed your approved **Ryuma concept** and **Kurogane-jō** art as reference images to the video tool (image-to-video) so the character/place stay on-model across shots.
- **The maple-red motif** (Kaede) should recur — leaves in 2, 3, 5 — then vanish after the murder; bring a single red leaf back at the very end as a ghost of her.
- **Withhold rule:** never show the killer's face or Sadao doing anything sinister — the whole story depends on it. Shot 8 must read as *pure comfort.*
- **The match cut (9→10→11)** child→adult→world is the spine of the edit; time everything to land it.
- **Faces are the hardest for AI video** — favor wider/atmospheric shots for generated clips and keep tight emotional close-ups (3, 9) for hand-crafted or carefully-referenced generation.
