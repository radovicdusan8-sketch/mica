# THE REALM OF AMAGIRI — World & Clans

> Companion to the [Story Bible](STORY_BIBLE.md). This doc builds the world *above* the road: the land, its history, the elemental clans, and the politics Ryuma walks into. Everything here serves the locked spine — it never contradicts it.

**Status:** First full pass (2026-07-31). Consistent with Story Bible v2. Proposed names flagged _[PROPOSED]_.

## 1. The Land

**Amagiri** — "Heaven's Mist" — a fractured realm of mountain valleys, lantern-lit cities, misted forests, and old battlefields where spirits linger. Once unified under a single banner; now a patchwork of clans, each holding its own domain and its own element.

The mist is not just weather. It is the realm's *nature* — Amagiri sits where the veil between the living and the dead runs thin, which is why spirits, oni, and cursed steel are real here and why the fifth power ever became possible. The higher and older the place, the thinner the veil: cliff-shrines, storm-peaks, and the deep hollows are where the dead still walk.

**Geography, roughly:** a long spine of mountains runs the realm north to south. The four elemental homelands sit at the compass points around a central lowland where **Kurogane-jō**, the smith-clan's castle, commands the crossroads. The road of the game spirals outward from that center and back — Ryuma is cast *out* into the realm and fights his way *home*.

## 2. The Fivefold Legend — History of Unification & Fracture

Everything about Ryuma's blade — why it matters, why it is feared — traces to one story every child in Amagiri knows.

- **The First Blade.** Centuries ago the realm was a hundred warring bloodlines. One warrior — remembered only as **the Fivefold Lord** _[personal name PROPOSED: Amatsu]_ — carried a single legendary katana, forged by the ancestral **Kurogane** smith, that held **all four elements at once**. No one could stand against a blade that was wind and water and fire and lightning by turns. The Fivefold Lord united Amagiri and ruled a golden age.
- **The Fifth, and the Fall.** In the myths the Fivefold Lord did not stop at four. To hold the realm forever, they reached for a **fifth power — the forbidden one** (see [Magic & Demon lore](MAGIC_AND_DEMON.md)) — and it devoured them. The golden age ended not in war but in a single night no chronicle will name. This is the seed of the taboo: *the fifth power is the shortcut to unity that always consumes the one who takes it.*
- **The Fracture.** When the Fivefold Lord died, no heir could hold more than **two** elements. The one legendary blade was lost or broken, and the realm split back into the elemental domains it holds to this day. The **Kurogane** smiths survived the collapse as neutral craftsmen — they made the blades for *every* clan, so every clan needed them, so no clan destroyed them.
- **The Prophecy.** *"When the Fivefold Blade is made whole, one hand shall bind Amagiri again."* To ambitious lords this is a threat: a true Fivefold heir would outrank them all overnight. To the suffering common folk it is hope. **Ryuma's katana is a reforging of that legend** — the one blade in generations that can hold four. That is why Tomoe hid its nature, why the teachers risk everything to awaken it, and why Sadao cannot let it live free.

> **Why this matters for the game:** Ryuma is not just a wronged son with a good sword. He is walking prophecy. Every clan lord who profits from the fracture has reason to fear or hunt him — which is how a personal revenge story earns a realm-sized stage.

## 3. How the Elemental Society Works

- **Affinity is inherited.** An elemental gift passes by blood. A bloodline is "of Wind" or "of Fire" the way a family is of a name.
- **One is common, two is a master, four is legend.** Most warriors of a gifted line channel **one** element. The rare prodigy channels **two** — this alone marks a master (Master Hidetora wielded **Fire & Lightning**). To hold all **four** belongs only to the Fivefold legend. This scarcity is the whole reason Ryuma's blade is a political earthquake.
- **The smith makes the channel.** A warrior does not simply *have* fire — an element must be **bound into steel** by a Kurogane-trained smith, and only then can a matching bloodline channel it. This is why the smith clan sits at the center of everything, and why **Old Tomoe** is the one hand alive who can awaken Ryuma's blade. (Full ritual & rules in [Magic & Demon lore](MAGIC_AND_DEMON.md).)
- **Clan = element = identity.** Because affinity runs in blood and steel, the domains organized around the elements long ago. To change your element is to betray your ancestors. This rigidity is exactly what makes a *fourfold* wanderer look like a walking heresy — and a savior.

## 4. The Clans of Amagiri

Six houses matter to the story. Four hold the elements; one holds the forge; one holds a grudge older than any of them.

### ⚙️ House Kurogane — "Black-Iron" — the Smith-Lords *(Ryuma's clan)*
Home: **Kurogane-jō**, the Iron Castle, at the realm's crossroads. Neither the largest army nor the proudest lineage — but the most *needed*. For generations the Kurogane have been the neutral kingmakers of Amagiri: guardians of the ancestral forge that made the Fivefold Blade, and the only house trusted to bind elements into the steel of all the others. Their bloodline is unusually gifted — enough to produce a two-element master like **Hidetora** — but their true power was always **trust**. Sadao's coup did not just steal a castle; it seized the one forge that legitimizes a Fivefold heir, and turned the realm's neutral heart into a weapon. *(Members: Hidetora, Kaede, Kaito, Ryuma, Sadao, Tomoe.)*

### 🌪️ House Sōra — "Sky" — Wind, at Takanoha _[PROPOSED name]_
Home: **Takanoha**, the Cliff of Bells. A reclusive, dwindling cliff-clan of falconers and bell-keepers who "see" by the wind. Once proud duelists; now withdrawn, keeping their high shrines and their silence. **Hazuki** is their blind bell-keeper and Wind teacher. Their withdrawal from realm politics is why Sadao has left them alone — for now — and why they can afford to help Ryuma.

### 🌊 House Nagi — "Sea-Calm" — Water, at Ukishima _[PROPOSED name]_
Home: **Ukishima**, the Floating Harbor. A pragmatic, mercantile clan of ferry-folk, traders, and soul-boatmen. Water people bend rather than break — which is both their strength and, under Sadao's pressure, their shame: many harbor-lords have quietly "accommodated" the new power at Kurogane-jō. **Isen**, who laid down his sword to ferry souls in penance, is their Water teacher and their conscience.

### 🔥 House Kaen — "Blaze" — Fire, at Homura _[PROPOSED name]_
Home: **Homura**, the Ember Forge. Furnace-lords and fire-warriors: proud, martial, hot-tempered, keepers of the great smelters that feed steel to the whole realm. If Kurogane are the artists of the blade, Kaen are its industry. **Genta** — Hidetora's sworn brother who fled the coup — drinks away his shame here and hands his fire to the son he abandoned.

### ⚡ House Ikazuchi — "Thunder" — Lightning, at Narukami _[PROPOSED name]_
Home: **Narukami**, the Storm Shrine. Once priest-warriors of the storm-peak and keepers of the realm's deepest lore — including the forbidden art of binding *souls* into steel. Generations ago they were broken and scattered for that heresy; today the house is all but extinct. The last true master is **the Hermit** _[personal name PROPOSED: Raizō]_ — Hidetora's own teacher, exiled for the same forbidden knowledge, and the only living man who remembers what the "demon" truly is. Lightning is thus the realm's *cursed, diminished* element — fitting that Ryuma learns it last, and that the demon truth traces back up this storm-peak.

---

### 🌫️ House Kiri — "the Mist" — the Rival, the Long Grudge _[PROPOSED name]_
Home: a poor, fog-drowned domain at the realm's edge _[PROPOSED: **Muonji**, "the silent temple-valley"]_. For the whole of recorded history, **Kiri were the weakest house in Amagiri** — a thin, unreliable elemental gift, small lands, overlooked at every council, the clan no one feared. That humiliation is their engine. Too weak to ever win by steel, they learned across generations to win by **patience and shadow**: to watch, to wait, to place their people unseen inside stronger houses, and to move like fog — you notice nothing, and then you cannot see your own hand.

Their oldest ambition is the one everyone dismissed them as too small to hold: to seize the **Fivefold legacy** — the forge, the legend, the realm — and rule where they were always scorned. **They have been plotting this for a very long time.** Their masterstroke was not an army; it was a single blade planted in the realm's most trusted heart: House Kurogane. That blade is **Sadao**. *(See §5, and Story Bible §5–6. The irony is the whole realm's: Amagiri, "Heaven's Mist," undone by the Mist.)*

## 5. The War for the Realm — Kiri's Long Con

Sadao is not a Kurogane elder who grew ambitious. **He is House Kiri's deepest-planted agent** — possibly their own heir — who spent a lifetime wearing the face of a loyal council-man to hollow out the clan from the inside. His coup is the payoff of a plot older than he is.

- **The claim (the mask):** to the council and the realm, Sadao presents himself as the **strong hand Amagiri needs** — the man who will end the long fracture where a failing old man could not. Many believe him. That mask is what makes him dangerous, and what makes the finale a matter of *truth*, not just swordplay — the clan must learn it was **conquered from outside and never knew it.**
- **The true aim:** Kiri does not want to lead the fracture — it wants to *end* it on Kiri's terms, holding the forge and, in time, the Fivefold Blade itself. A true Fivefold heir is the one thing that unravels the whole generational plot, which is why Ryuma must die or be discredited.
- **The method:** clan by clan, Sadao cows or co-opts the elemental houses — pressure here, a bounty there, soldiers garrisoned in a "protected" city, a wavering lord quietly bought. The road Ryuma walks is a realm *in the act of falling* to a shadow no one has yet named.
- **The map of loyalty (drives the city states):**
  - **Occupied / under his thumb:** **Higan** (Water region, festival city), **Kareno** (the Ashlands, refugees fleeing his soldiers), and of course **Kurogane-jō** itself.
  - **Pressured but standing:** **Ukishima**'s harbor-lords are wavering; **Homura** simmers.
  - **Free and defiant:** **Shirasagi**, the Heron Castle — the last great city that openly refuses Sadao, and so the natural place for Ryuma's growing legend to gather allies.
  - **Beyond his reach:** the reclusive **Takanoha** and the near-empty **Narukami** — too remote, too spent to bother with. Which is exactly why the old teachers there are still free to help.
- **The thing he fears:** a true Fivefold heir. A wanderer awakening all four elements is the one event that can turn the co-opted clans against him overnight — because it makes Ryuma, not Sadao, the answer to the prophecy. This is why the bounty on Ryuma climbs with every element he wins, and why the finale is Sadao trying to convince the very council that would otherwise flock to the Fivefold heir.

## 6. Spirits, Oni & the Thin Veil

Amagiri is haunted, not historical. The mist marks where the living world and the dead press close.

- **Yōkai & spirits** roam the wilds and the old battlefields — most are hazards or bosses (the **Tengu** of Takanoha, the drowned **Funayūrei** of Ukishima, the **Fire Oni** of Homura, the storm-beast **Raiju** of Narukami). Some are neutral, some grieving, some malevolent.
- **The dead linger** where the veil is thin: lantern-festivals for the dead (Higan), soul-ferries across black water (Ukishima), and the haunted soul-binding ruins of **Yomotsu**, the Hollow, where the forbidden art was practiced and where Ryuma finally faces the truth of the fifth power.
- **This is the world's promise and its dread:** a place where a mother's soul *can* be bound into a blade — the tragedy the whole story turns on is only possible because Amagiri is the kind of world it is.
