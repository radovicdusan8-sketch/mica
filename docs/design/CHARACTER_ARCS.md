# CHARACTER ARCS — Development Across the Journey

> How each character *changes* over the game — trajectories, turning points, and relationships — tied to the road. Pairs with [CHARACTER_DESIGN.md](CHARACTER_DESIGN.md) (who they are) and [STORY_BEATS.md](STORY_BEATS.md) (where it happens). Consistent with the locked spine.

**Status:** First pass (2026-07-31).

**The through-line for the whole cast:** *everyone is defined by the night the family fell.* Ryuma walks his father's life to become his father's heir; the teachers are pieces of Hidetora working out their debts; Sadao is the wound wearing a smile. The journey is one grief, refracted.

---

## RYUMA — from dismissed son to the truth's heir

The spine of the game. His arc runs on **three engines at once**: grief, power, and a mystery.

- **Start — the dismissed son.** Powerless, grieving, defined by others: too young to be heir, a brother he lost, a father just murdered. He carries a half-sentence and no idea who he is without his family.
- **Act I (Wind → Water → Fire) — son to swordsman, grief to investigation.** Each teacher gives him power *and* a piece of the truth: Hazuki's first doubt, Isen's "your father suspected," Genta's shame. He stops merely mourning and starts *building a case.* His legend grows faster than his understanding.
- **Act II (Lightning + the fragment) — into and past the father.** Mastering Fire and Lightning, he stands where Hidetora stood, then beyond — right as the second fragment and its dread arrive. The boy who was "too young" is now the most dangerous blade in Amagiri, and it frightens him.
- **Act III (the Demon, the reunion) — the truth, and the temptation.** He gets his brother, the whole truth, and a weapon made of his mother's soul. The demon *tempts* him to burn her for power — his lowest, rawest point.
- **Act IV (the return) — earned restraint.** His growth resolves not in a bigger explosion but in **choosing not to spend her** — letting Kaede be a mother, not a weapon. He reclaims the clan by winning back a broken people, not just killing a villain.
- **End — the heir who finished the sentence.** He becomes what his father hoped: not the avenger, but the son who brought the family home. Triumphant, and marked by loss.

**Core tension:** power vs. love. The whole progression system is built so the *player feels* the pull to over-use the demon — so Ryuma's restraint is the player's, too.

## KAITO — from branded exile to vindicated brother

- **Start (offscreen, cold open) — the golden heir destroyed.** Framed for his mother's murder, cast out, abandoned (as he sees it) by a family that didn't fight for him. Years of exile forge contempt over an open wound.
- **Reputation (mid-game) — the fearsome rogue.** Ryuma chases a legend of a bitter, deadly swordsman before he ever meets the man. Breadcrumbs (Isen, Kareno, the Enforcer) build dread and sympathy.
- **The reunion — from enemy to brother.** Their first meeting is a **duel** (Kaito tests/rejects him, fire against fire) that becomes a **back-to-back stand** when Kuroba's men close in. Surviving together cracks the armor.
- **The truth — from bitterness to purpose.** When the fragment reveals their mother and the whole design, Kaito's contempt breaks — he was *never* abandoned; he was *stolen* from the family. Exile becomes a mission.
- **End — vindicated, co-heir.** Cleared before the whole council, he stands with Ryuma in their father's seat. The wound closes; the brother is home.

**Development note:** Kaito is who Ryuma could have become — abandoned to bitterness. Their arcs are a mirror; the brother-bond is the game's heart.

## THE BROTHER-BOND — the emotional core

The relationship *is* the story. Track it as its own arc:
1. **Absence** (Prologue): a name no one will speak; a wound.
2. **Pursuit** (Acts I–II): Ryuma chases a legend, assembling a brother out of rumor and dread.
3. **Collision** (reunion): distrust, a real duel, forced alliance.
4. **Truth** (the fragment): the lie that separated them exposed — they were torn apart *on purpose.*
5. **Union** (finale): two blades, their mother's between them, home together.

> The tragedy the villain built was *keeping these two apart.* The triumph is their reunion — everything else serves it.

## SADAO — the arc that happens in the *player's* head

Sadao barely changes; **the player's understanding of him does.** His "arc" is a controlled demolition of trust.
1. **Beloved** (Prologue): the warm uncle who grieves hardest, pulls Ryuma from the fire, holds the house together. The player *likes* him.
2. **Absent benefactor** (mid-game): his comforting letters are Ryuma's anchor from afar — reassuring, candid, fatherly.
3. **Uneasy** (mid–late): Ayame's whispers and the world's wrongness gnaw; the player suspects before Ryuma will.
4. **The murderer** (reunion): revealed as the author of everything — the framing, both parents' deaths, the hollowed clan.
5. **The outsider** (finale): the deepest cut — he was never even Kurogane, but **Kiri**, a planted blade playing a generational con.

**Design intent:** every warm Sadao moment must be sincere enough that the reveal makes the player *re-examine* it — the letters re-readable, the grief re-watchable. He never rants; to the end he *believes* he was the realm's necessary hand.

## KAEDE — the wound that becomes the weapon that becomes peace

Present as memory and spirit; her arc is thematic.
- **The wound** (backstory): murdered for seeing the truth; her death framed on her own son — the family's original injury.
- **The secret** (mid-game): unknowingly, Ryuma carries her — the "demon" in the fragment.
- **The temptation** (Demon): her power is offered as fuel; using her risks losing her forever.
- **The witness** (finale): given the choice to be a weapon, she chooses to be a *mother and a truth-teller* — she testifies, clears Kaito, damns Sadao. The wound the family feared becomes their salvation.
- **Peace:** her purpose served, she passes on — lost a second time, but at rest.

---

## AYAME — loyalty tested to the edge

- **Start:** Kaede's handmaid, Ryuma's childhood friend; stayed inside Kurogane-jō after the fall, never believing Kaito's guilt.
- **The spy** (from Higan): risks everything to feed Ryuma the truth from inside the enemy's house — the counterweight to Sadao's lies.
- **Captured** (mid–late): her line goes dark; taken and jailed — a gut-drop and a debt.
- **Freed** (finale): the brothers break her out; she survives as a **living witness** who helps turn the broken clan.
- **End:** one thread of the old life that endures — a bittersweet light. *(Deeper bond with Ryuma or steadfast loyalty — open, unforced.)*

---

## SŌJIRŌ — the growth-mirror (recurring rival)

- **Sakaimichi:** duels Ryuma for joy, unimpressed but intrigued — *"You're not finished yet."*
- **Higan:** reappears, half-tests / half-helps; measuring Ryuma's climb.
- **Shirasagi:** the **decisive duel** — Ryuma finally meets him as an equal; Sōjirō, satisfied at last, turns **ally.**
- **Finale:** stands with the brothers. His arc is a yardstick: every bout shows how far Ryuma has come, and his eventual respect *certifies* Ryuma's growth.

## THE FOUR TEACHERS — one collective arc (Hidetora's life, walked)

Each teacher is a chapter of the father, and helping Ryuma is each one **settling a debt** — so the journey is Ryuma meeting his father through the people who loved and failed him.
- **Hazuki (Wind):** the youth-rival who owes Hidetora her life; pays it forward, and plants the first doubt.
- **Isen (Water):** the war-comrade who owes an unpayable debt; hands Ryuma the truth of his father's suspicion.
- **Genta (Fire):** the sworn brother who *failed* Hidetora and atones by giving his son the family flame.
- **Raizō (Lightning):** the old master who taught Hidetora and knows the forbidden truth; his guilt (the soul-art) is the key to the demon.

Together they trace **youth → war → brotherhood → mastery** — Hidetora's whole life, so that by Narukami, Ryuma has met his father four times over and is ready to surpass him.
