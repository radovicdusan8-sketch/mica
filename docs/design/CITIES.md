# THE ROAD OF AMAGIRI — The 13 Stops, Deepened

> Companion to the [Story Bible](STORY_BIBLE.md) §9 and the [World of Amagiri](WORLD_AMAGIRI.md). This doc turns each one-line stop into a *place*: look, culture, faction state, key NPCs, boss, a side-thread, and what it does for story + mechanics. Consistent with the locked spine.

**Status:** First full pass (2026-07-31). The itinerary (order, powers, major events) is locked; the texture below is open to shape.

**Rhythm reminder:** the road alternates **Element cities** (learn a power, fight a boss) with **Interlude cities** (master the power, farm skill points, advance the plot). Powers are spaced so each is mastered before the next arrives; the Demon comes two full arcs past Lightning.

**Art direction reminder:** *cities & environments* are **stylized & painterly** — atmospheric, ominous, overkill-detailed. *Characters* are **grounded and realistic**. Grounded heroes in a painted mythic world.

---

## P — Kurogane-jō · The Iron Castle *(Prologue — no power)*
**Look:** black-iron ramparts wreathed in forge-smoke, banners of House Kurogane over a crossroads valley. The realm's neutral heart — until tonight.
**Culture:** the smith-lords' seat; halls of the ancestral forge that made the Fivefold Blade.
**Faction:** falls this chapter. By dawn it is **Sadao's**.
**Beat:** *The Fall.* The council doubts the aging Hidetora and dismisses Ryuma as heir. Sadao's long game closes — betrayal, and Hidetora is struck down. Dying, he gives Ryuma not a command to avenge but a road: **"Find your brother. Only together can you take back what is ours."** Ryuma flees with an un-awakened blade.
**Hook:** the man who gave the order stands calm amid the ruin, already the clan's new master.
**Teaches the player:** movement and base combat with **zero magic** — exactly Ryuma's starting state (maps to build Milestones 0–3).

## 1 — Takanoha · The Cliff of Bells *(Element — Learn WIND)*
**Look:** wind-carved cliffs strung with thousands of prayer-bells and hawk-lines; painterly white sky, everything in motion, the sound of bells everywhere.
**Culture:** House **Sōra**, reclusive falconers and bell-keepers who "see" by the wind. Withdrawn from realm politics — which is why they're still free.
**Faction:** beyond Sadao's reach (too remote, too spent).
**Key NPC — Hazuki (Wind teacher):** blind bell-keeper. Trained beside Hidetora in youth as an equal until a duel took her eyes; he carried her from the field and swore she'd never want for anything. **Helps because she owes his father her life** — and Ryuma's grip is Hidetora's ghost. She once met Lady Kaede and drops **the first crack in the lie**: her death was no accident.
**Boss — the Tengu:** a wind-spirit guardian of the high shrine; the trial that awakens Wind. Technique: ***Severing Gale***.
**Side-thread:** the hawks of Takanoha tie to **Kaze**, Ryuma's messenger bird (his father's boyhood gift) — introduce or re-bond the hawk here.
**Does for the game:** first cinematic boss + first element awakened (maps to build Milestones 4–5).

---

## 2 — Sakaimichi · The Crossroads Market *(Interlude — Master WIND)*
**Look:** a sprawling, lantern-strung market town where every road in Amagiri meets; painted crowds, smoke, noise, danger under the color.
**Culture:** neutral trade-town, no clan's and everyone's; where news, coin, and blades change hands.
**Faction:** contested — Sadao's writ arrives here.
**Key beats:** **Old Tomoe's forge is the hub** for this stretch — gear, upgrades, and the man who begins to tell Ryuma what his blade truly is. **Sadao brands Ryuma a fugitive** and posts a bounty (the price will climb with every element). The **Rival, Sōjirō Amano "the Laughing Blade,"** first appears — a grinning duelist who fights for joy and takes Ryuma's measure.
**Side-thread:** economy & gear systems open here; first optional bounties/duels to farm **skill points** and master the Wind tree.
**Does for the game:** teaches mastery, economy, and the recurring-rival loop.

---

## 3 — Ukishima · The Floating Harbor *(Element — Learn WATER)*
**Look:** a city of boardwalks and boats over black water, fog on the tide, painted lanterns doubling in the surface; beautiful and mournful.
**Culture:** House **Nagi**, ferry-folk, traders, and soul-boatmen who carry the dead across the black water.
**Faction:** pressured — harbor-lords are quietly "accommodating" Sadao; the shame hangs over the docks.
**Key NPC — Isen (Water teacher):** bled beside Hidetora in the border wars; when Isen's blade failed and men died, Hidetora shouldered the blame *with* him. Isen laid down his sword to ferry souls in penance. **Helps because that debt is never paid.** He reveals the father **suspected betrayal** before the end.
**Boss — the Funayūrei:** drowned spirits that swarm the ferry-lanes; the trial that awakens Water. Technique: ***Returning Tide***.
**Side-thread:** the soul-ferry seeds the demon lore — souls *can* cross, souls *can* linger — foreshadowing Yomotsu and the fragment.
**Does for the game:** second element; deepens the conspiracy from "accident" to "betrayal."

---

## 4 — Higan · The Lantern City *(Interlude — Master WATER)*
**Look:** a whole city aglow for the **Festival of the Dead** — rivers of floating lanterns, masked crowds, painted night. Gorgeous cover for danger.
**Culture:** a Water-region city that honors the dead; the festival makes everyone a stranger in a mask.
**Faction:** **occupied — under Sadao's thumb.** Ryuma must move **incognito** through his enemy's city.
**Key NPC — Ayame (the Spy), first contact:** Lady Kaede's former handmaid *and* Ryuma's childhood friend, who stayed inside Kurogane-jō after the fall and never believed Kaito's guilt. From within Sadao's household she begins feeding Ryuma the truth — troop movements, plans, whispers of the mother's death. Her closeness to both Kaede and Ryuma makes the intel — and the danger — personal. **Kaze the hawk** begins carrying their letters.
**Recurring:** the **Rival** returns for a second bout.
**Side-thread:** a stealth/incognito flavor mission through a hostile city; meet an **ally** among the festival-goers.
**Does for the game:** turns the plot from personal grief toward active conspiracy; opens the letter/objective system via Kaze.

---

## 5 — Homura · The Ember Forge *(Element — Learn FIRE)*
**Look:** a city built into active smelters and lava-lit foundries; painted heat-haze, orange sky, the ring of a thousand hammers. Ominous grandeur.
**Culture:** House **Kaen**, furnace-lords and fire-warriors — the realm's steel industry; proud, martial, hot-tempered.
**Faction:** pressured but simmering — proud enough to resent Sadao, not yet brave enough to move.
**Key NPC — Genta (Fire teacher):** Hidetora's **sworn brother** by blood-oath, who — cornered and afraid when the coup came — **fled instead of standing** with his brother, and has drowned in shame and drink at the forge since. **Helps to atone**, handing his fire to the son he abandoned. This is the road's rawest father-bond beat.
**Boss — the Fire Oni:** a furnace-demon loosed in the deep smelters; the trial that awakens Fire — the **first of the father's two elements**. Technique: ***Ember Fang***.
**Side-thread:** Genta's confession of the night he ran; optional forge-trials for Fire-tree points.
**Does for the game:** third element; begins Ryuma stepping into his father's power.

---

## 6 — Kareno · The Ashlands *(Interlude — Master FIRE)*
**Look:** a scorched refugee sprawl on old battlefields — ash drifting like snow, painted grey skies, tents among ruins. Where Sadao's "unity" shows its true cost.
**Culture:** the displaced — common folk fleeing Sadao's soldiers. No clan; only survivors.
**Faction:** **occupied / militarized** — Sadao's troops press the refugees.
**Key beats:** Ryuma **defends refugees** from Sadao's soldiers and, in doing so, **becomes a legend to the common folk** — the first time the realm sees the Fivefold heir as a savior, not a fugitive. He faces **Sadao's Enforcer** for the first time and **picks up Kaito's trail.**
**Recurring:** **Kuroba "the Ironhound,"** Sadao's relentless hunter, is introduced here — cold, tireless, never stops tracking.
**Side-thread:** protect/escort missions that build Ryuma's public legend (a reputation the finale will cash in).
**Does for the game:** turns Ryuma from hunted fugitive into rising legend; introduces the mini-antagonist hunter.

---

## 7 — Narukami · The Storm Shrine *(Element — Learn LIGHTNING)*
**Look:** a shattered mountain shrine in permanent storm — black clouds, chained lightning, painted rain, ruined halls of a broken house. The realm's cursed, emptied peak.
**Culture:** House **Ikazuchi**, the storm-priests — all but extinct, broken generations ago for the forbidden soul-binding lore. Silence and ghosts.
**Faction:** beyond Sadao's reach — nothing left here to seize.
**Key NPC — the Hermit (Lightning teacher)** _[Raizō]_**:** **Hidetora's own master**, the first to put lightning in his hands; later exiled for the binding of souls into steel. **Helps because Ryuma is his student's son**, and he alone remembers what the "demon" truly is — a guilt he carries up the storm-peak.
**Boss — the Raiju:** a storm-beast prowling the ruined shrine; the trial that awakens Lightning — the **second of the father's elements, now surpassing him.** Technique: ***Thunderstep***.
**Key beat:** the **Mysterious Figure** delivers the **second fragment** here, and the Hermit points Ryuma toward **Kaito.**
**Does for the game:** fourth element (Ryuma is now near-Fivefold); hands over the fragment that carries the whole endgame.

---

## 8 — Shirasagi · The Heron Castle *(Interlude — Master LIGHTNING + upgrade all four)*
**Look:** a white castle-city like a heron on the water — elegant, defiant, banners flying; the one bright, unbowed place in a darkening realm.
**Culture:** the **last great free city** that openly refuses Sadao — a gathering point for the discontented.
**Faction:** **free and defiant** — and knows it may not stay that way.
**Key beats:** Ryuma's **growing legend draws allies** to his cause here — the "gather your strength" arc before the endgame. The **Rival's decisive duel** lands: Sōjirō, having measured Ryuma across the whole road, fights him for real — and likely stands with him after.
**Side-thread:** recruit named allies; unlock cross-element **upgrades for all four** trees; the war-council mood.
**Does for the game:** the coalition forms; the last pure "grow and prepare" beat before the point of no return.

---

## 9 — Sekimon · The Iron Gate *(Interlude — Mastery gauntlet, all elements)*
**Look:** a colossal fortress pass choking the only road home — iron gates, killing-slopes, painted storm-light. The wall between Ryuma and Kurogane-jō.
**Culture:** a garrison-fortress, Sadao's forward wall; no civilians, only soldiers.
**Faction:** **hard Sadao stronghold** — the point of no return.
**Key beat:** Ryuma **storms the pass** and **ends Sadao's Enforcer** in a mastery gauntlet that demands all four elements. **Dying, the Enforcer reveals a truth** that drives Ryuma into the Hollow — the last push toward the fifth power.
**Side-thread:** an all-elements combat crucible; the game's skill check before the demon and the finale.
**Does for the game:** proves Ryuma's fourfold mastery; the door closes behind him.

---

## 10 — Yomotsu · The Hollow *(Interlude — Awaken DEMON)*
**Look:** haunted ruins where the veil is thinnest — a grey underworld-place of drifting spirits and old soul-binding altars; painted, dreadful, beautiful. Where the forbidden art was practiced.
**Culture:** no living culture — only the dead and the ruins of the Ikazuchi's heresy.
**Faction:** none; the realm itself, at its thinnest.
**Key beat:** Ryuma **confronts a vision of his mother** and **learns the demon's cost** — power that devours as it grants, that can lose her forever or overtake him. He awakens the fifth. *(Handle the reveal exactly as staged — the demon **is** Kaede — per Story Bible §6 and the [Magic doc](MAGIC_AND_DEMON.md) §5.)*
**Side-thread:** the demon resource and its risk mechanics come fully online here — and the game must make the player *feel* the temptation to burn her for power.
**Does for the game:** the fifth power; the emotional core of the endgame is set.

---

## 11 — Kaito's Refuge *(Reunion — the brother)*
**Look:** a hidden holdfast of a hunted man — spare, defensible, far from any clan; painted wilderness closing around it.
**Culture:** none but Kaito and whatever hard company an exiled swordsman keeps.
**Key NPC — Kaito (the brother):** once the clan's brightest blade and rightful heir, **framed for their mother's death** and exiled; now a rogue with a fearsome, bitter reputation, certain his own blood abandoned him.
**The meeting (open decision — see below):** the reunion is the heart of the game.
**The revelation:** when Ryuma awakens the fragment, the truth pours out through Kaede — **Kaito was framed, Sadao murdered her, Sadao holds the clan.** And Kaito, who has watched from outside with no illusions, tears down the last one: he tells Ryuma **what has truly become of home** — the elders purged, the believers broken, the clan hollowed into a frightened tool — flatly contradicting every comforting letter Ryuma believed. The friend he entrusted the clan to has been its wolf, and Ryuma's trust and absence *enabled* it. Exile becomes purpose; two brothers torn apart *on purpose* choose to walk home together, their mother's blade between them. *(The letters-deception thread pays off here — see [HOME_FRONT.md](HOME_FRONT.md).)*
**Does for the game:** the emotional turn; the party becomes two blades pointed home.

> **Recommended for the open "first meeting" question:** stage it as **a duel that becomes a stand shoulder-to-shoulder.** Kaito, bitter and unbelieving, tests Ryuma with steel first (a real fight) — until **Kuroba the Ironhound** and Sadao's men close in and force the brothers back-to-back. Surviving *together* is what buys the fragile trust that the fragment's truth then seals. This delivers both beats the Bible floats, in the order that earns the reunion. _(Provisional — easy to revisit.)_

## R — Return to Kurogane-jō · The Finale *(Twist → Triumph)*
**Look:** the black-iron castle again — but as a siege, storm-lit, the painted world holding its breath.
**The hollow welcome:** this is *not* a hero's return. Sadao's long purge (see [HOME_FRONT.md](HOME_FRONT.md)) has left the clan cowed and hollow — believers broken, elders gone, some folk even resenting the heir who left and empowered Sadao while they suffered. Ryuma comes home to fear, not cheers; reclaiming the clan means winning back a broken people who no longer believe.
**Freeing Ayame:** in the assault, the brothers break **Ayame** out of Sadao's cells — the mid-game debt paid; she survives as a living witness to his rule and adds her voice to Kaede's testimony.
**Key beats:** the brothers march home. The elders who followed Sadao are **not all villains** — many believed the lie. The finale confronts **Sadao**, who argues to the last that he *saved* the clan from a failing old man.
**The twist that turns triumphant:** at the height of it — Ryuma tempted to spend the demon recklessly and lose his mother forever — **Kaede's bound spirit instead reveals the truth to the whole council.** The very "demon" the clan feared becomes the witness that **clears Kaito and damns Sadao.** The family's greatest wound becomes the weapon that wins. Sadao falls; the clan is reclaimed; Kaito is vindicated at last. Her purpose served, **Kaede passes on in peace,** and the brothers stand together in the seat their father meant for them both.
**Earned grief note:** triumphant, not clean — the father is gone; the mother, having spoken her truth, is lost a second time; the clan restored and forever changed.
**Does for the game:** pays off every system and thread — the fourfold blade, the demon's restraint, the legend, the coalition, the brothers.

## Recurring Cast Across the Road *(quick reference)*
- **Sōjirō Amano — "the Laughing Blade"** (Rival): Sakaimichi → Higan → **decisive duel at Shirasagi** → likely ally. A measure of Ryuma's growth; very Zoro.
- **Kuroba — "the Ironhound"** (Hunter): introduced at **Kareno** → forces the reunion fight at Kaito's Refuge → **last stand at Sekimon**. Cold, tireless.
- **Ayame — the Spy:** first contact at **Higan**; from inside Kurogane-jō she feeds Ryuma the *truth* — the counterpoint to Sadao's comforting letters — until she is **captured** and her line goes dark. **The brothers free her in the finale.** (See [HOME_FRONT.md](HOME_FRONT.md).)
- **Kaze — the Loyal Bird:** Ryuma's messenger hawk, his father's gift; the same bird carries **Sadao's warm lies and Ayame's desperate truth** in counterpoint, opens objectives, and lets home react to Ryuma's legend. Tied to the hawks of Takanoha.
- **Sadao — from afar:** while Ryuma travels, the clan is (disastrously) in Sadao's hands; his periodic **letters** report a thriving clan with small "hiccups" he's handling — the kindly lie over the purge back home. (See [HOME_FRONT.md](HOME_FRONT.md).)
- **The Mysterious Figure:** glimpsed across cities; delivers the second fragment at **Narukami.**
- **Old Tomoe — the Swordsmith:** the hub/guide who awakens the blade and keeps the deepest secret.
